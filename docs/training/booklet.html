Before You Start

You'll need the following before your first match:

Your Open Door Live loginThe email and password from your welcome email.
The app, installedOn the phone you'll actually stream from.
Your streaming kitFull kit for Home Ground schools, or your own device & data for Kickoff.
A stable data connectionAt the venue — this is what your stream actually runs on.
Two places, one login.

The same email and password sign you in to both the Open Door Live website (fixtures, teams, sponsors) and the Crew Sign-In inside the app (match day). No separate accounts needed.

Logging In for the First Time

Website

Open the welcome email from Open Door Live and click the link inside it.

You'll land on the Set Password page. Choose a password and confirm it.

You're signed in and taken straight to your Admin Panel.

Lost the email, or the link has expired? Open Door Live can resend it at any time — see Support (Section 10).

3

The Website — Your Admin Panel

This is where your school sets everything up before a match: your logo, your teams, your fixtures, and your sponsors. A school account only ever sees its own school's information here.

3.1 Upload Your Logo & Confirm Consent

Website

Go to School in the admin menu.

Upload your school's logo (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5MB).

Tick the consent confirmation — that your school holds appropriate parental/guardian consent to film and broadcast its students.

Do this first.

You cannot create a fixture until consent is confirmed — the website blocks it on purpose. A one-time step, not something you'll repeat every match.

3.2 Add Your Teams

Website

Go to Teams in the admin menu.

Click + New Team.

Enter the team's full name, and — optionally — a short name for the scoreboard (e.g. "Rev High 1st" for "Revelation High 1st Team").

Save.

You need at least two teams on file before you can create a fixture.

3.3 Create a Fixture (Schedule a Match)

Website

Go to Fixtures and click + New Fixture.

Choose the sport, the home team, the away team, and the kickoff date and time.

Save.

This automatically sets up the YouTube broadcast behind the scenes — the fixture shows "Provisioning…" for a moment, then "Ready to stream." No further action needed.

3.4 Set Up Sponsors

Website
  • Go to Sponsors and click + New Sponsor to add one (name, tier, position, an optional click-through link, and a logo).
  • Open the fixture you want to sponsor and use Assign Sponsor to place one on that specific match, in a chosen position.

A sponsor only needs to be created once — after that, assign it to as many fixtures as you like.

3.5 After the Match — Marking It Completed

Website

Usually automatic — see Section 7 for how confirming "Mark completed" in the app does this for you. If it wasn't confirmed there, or a score needs correcting:

Open the fixture's page in the admin panel.

Under Match Result, enter the final score (or just confirm completion for a Clean Slate/Event fixture, which has no score).

Save. The match moves to the Completed tab and shows its final score.

This can be re-done any time if a score needs correcting after the fact.

3.6 Taking Down a Video (If Ever Needed)

Website

A fixture's page has a Take down video button. This immediately stops the video showing on the Open Door Live website — but the underlying YouTube video isn't removed, since match footage is public on YouTube. For a full YouTube takedown, contact Open Door Live directly — see Support.

4

Match-Day Roles & Logistics

Streaming a match well is as much about arriving prepared as it is about the app itself. This is general guidance to adapt, not a fixed rulebook — set whatever version works for your own staff and schedule.

4.1 Who's Involved

The OperatorRuns the phone and app during the match — staff, a trained student under supervision, or an Open Door Live crew member if you've booked coverage.
Backup operatorWorth having a second person who knows the basics. Open Door Live currently issues one login per school — ask for a second if you need one.
School contactDeals with Open Door Live on consent, sponsors, and reports. Doesn't need to be the operator.

4.2 – 4.4 The Match-Day Timeline

Before kickoff

  • Charge the whole kit the night before.
  • Arrive 20–30 min early — setup takes longer than it looks.
  • Position the tripod for a clear, elevated view, away from backlight.
  • Test the data signal at that exact spot.
  • Sign in, load the fixture, frame the shot (Section 5).

During the match

  • Stay with the kit — never leave the phone unattended once live.
  • Watch the status chip (Section 6).
  • Use Next Period, Mute, and the timer as the match unfolds.
  • Something wrong? Don't panic — see Troubleshooting (Section 8).

After the match

  • End the stream and confirm the result (Section 7) while it's fresh.
  • Pack the kit away carefully — good care keeps your renewal rate down.
  • Recharge everything for next time.

4.5 Covering Multiple Events on the Same Day

One kit and one operator can't cover two sports or events at once. Extra Kit (a second full streaming kit) or booking Crew for the day / Match Day Pro are the two ways to cover it — arrange either ahead of time, since availability isn't guaranteed on short notice.

The App — Setting Up to Stream

The first time you open the app, grant it camera and microphone permission when asked — it can't stream without both.

Scoreboard iconLive control panel — score, timer, GO LIVE / END STREAM.

5.1 Sign In and Load Your Fixture

App

Tap the gear (settings) icon.

On the Stream Setup tab, under CREW SIGN-IN, enter your email and password and tap Sign In.

Choose your fixture from the dropdown list.

Tap Load Fixture.

Load Fixture fills in everything for you: the stream connection details, both team names, the sport, the home team's logo, and any sponsor logos assigned to that fixture. A sponsor with no logo yet is simply left as-is — the stream still works fine.

5.2 Double-Check Before Kickoff

App
  • Sports tab — confirm the sport and team names. Edit either by hand if needed.
  • Sponsor Ads tab — check the four logo slots (top-right logo, lower-third headline sponsor, two bottom corners). Pick a different image any time.
  • Camera tab — resolution (720p/1080p) and stream quality (Data saver/Standard/High). A mid-stream change only applies next time the app opens.

5.3 Framing Your Shot

App

The zoom slider on the left edge of the preview is always available — no menu needed. It runs from a wide 0.6x up to 5x, and gently snaps to exactly 1.0x (marked with a small tick).

The App — Going Live & Scoring the Match

6.1 Starting the Stream

App

Tap the scoreboard icon to open the live control panel.

Tap GO LIVE.

Watch the status chip: CONNECTING… then LIVE once the stream is up (can take up to a minute).

If this fixture was loaded via Crew Sign-In, going live also marks it LIVE on the website automatically.

6.2 Scoring — Rugby, Soccer, Netball, Hockey, Tennis

Rugby

Try +5Con +2Pen +3Drop +3 + / − plainUndoSwapNext Period

Soccer, Netball, Hockey, Tennis

+ / − plainUndoSwapNext Period

Next Period moves 1st Half → 2nd Half (or 1st–4th Quarter for netball) — manual, on purpose. Tennis's running score is sets won, not points.

6.3 Scoring — Cricket

Runs, wickets & overs — its own scoreboard

012346 Wd/Nb +1Bye +1WICKET Swap InningsReset Innings

Overs advance automatically on legal balls. Wickets cap at 10. Swap Innings shows the target/chase line.

6.4 – 6.6 Timer, Mute & Reconnecting

The timerStart and Pause control the match clock in the live control panel.
Mute audioCuts sound only — video and connection keep going. Every new stream starts unmuted.
If the connection drops

The app reconnects on its own — you'll see RECONNECTING… (attempt N). Stay on the stream, don't close the app. Only tap Stop Reconnecting if you genuinely want to end the broadcast.

Ending the Stream

App
Tap END STREAMIn the live control panel.
"Match finished?" dialogRugby/Soccer/Netball/Hockey/Tennis, loaded via Crew Sign-In, shows the final score.
Mark completedFinishes the match on the website with that score.
Just stop streamingMatch isn't over yet — stream again afterward.
Cricket and Clean Slate/Event.

No automatic dialog — cricket because only the current innings is tracked, Clean Slate/Event because there's no score to record. Mark these completed manually from the website afterward (Section 3.5).

Troubleshooting

Signing in & permissions

Camera/mic permission blocked.
Phone Settings → Apps → Open Door Live → Permissions — allow both Camera and Microphone.
"Backend not configured."
Crew Sign-In isn't available on this device. Contact Open Door Live — or enter a stream URL/key by hand if you've been given one directly.
"Sign in failed."
Double-check your email and password. Forgotten it? Contact Open Door Live to have it reset.
Can't sign in, need a new invite link.
Use "Lost an invite, or can't sign in?" on the website, or contact Open Door Live directly.

Fixtures & sponsors

No fixtures in the Load Fixture dropdown.
It may not be created yet, or kickoff has passed. Check with whoever manages fixtures, or create it yourself (Section 3.3).
Load Fixture skipped a sponsor logo.
That sponsor has no logo uploaded, or wasn't assigned to this fixture. The stream still works — add it manually on Sponsor Ads if needed.

Connection & status chip

Stuck on CONNECTING… a long time.
Check your data signal first — up to a minute is normal. Still stuck? Try loading the fixture again, or double-check URL/key if entered by hand.
RECONNECTING… (attempt N).
Normal after a signal drop — the app retries on its own. Stay on the stream, don't close the app. See Section 6.6.
FAILED — won't connect at all.
Usually a wrong stream key. Loaded via Crew Sign-In? Try loading it again. Entered by hand? Double-check URL and key.
LIVE badge not showing on the website.
Best-effort and can lag a little — give it a minute. Never affects the stream itself.

After the match

Match not moving to the Completed tab.
Still needs marking completed — confirm "Mark completed" at End Stream (two-team sports), or manually on the website (Section 3.5).
Final score shown is wrong.
Go to the fixture's page on the website and re-enter it (Section 3.5) — correctable any time.
Settings look different on another phone.
Saved to the device, not your account — each phone remembers its own setup.
A parent can't watch a match.
They need a signed-in parent account first. Then "Watch on YouTube" opens the stream directly.
